GETTING AROUND

  • Turn moves: between pages, along a list, or changes a number you have opened.
  • Click acts on what you are looking at. On GAUGES, a click opens that gauge big; click again to come back.
  • Hold about a second goes back one step. On a page that has its own settings (the clock, GAUGES, TEMP, SOLAR) a hold opens them.
  • Go home from anywhere: hold the knob, turn two clicks, let go.
  • Erasing anything is confirmed the same way everywhere: hold and watch the bar fill. Let go early and nothing happens.
