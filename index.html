ONE KNOB, EVERY PAGE

Turn to change pages, click to open one, hold for its menu. That is the whole manual.

Gauges

Speed, rpm, coolant and volts from the engine computer, each with its own scale and warning colours.

Speed, big

Click a gauge and it fills the screen, with top, average and the drive so far beside it.

Temperatures

Outside, inside, fridge and freezer from wired probes. The fridge warns you before the food does.

Weather from pressure

Three days of barometer, a trend line, and a storm warning when the pressure falls a storm's worth.

Solar and house battery

Watts in, amps, charge state, over Bluetooth from the solar controller and the shunt.

Health over weeks

Crank volts, charging, fuel trim, coolant and mileage as small graphs of recent drives, so a slow problem shows early.

Tilt for camp

Pitch and roll from a tilt sensor, and a camp view that tells you which wheel to lift.

Trips and fuel

Two trip meters, the tank, range, and a fill-up prompt that learns where you buy fuel.

WHAT IT WATCHES FOR

The clock reads the engine computer and the probes all the time and only interrupts you when it matters.

  • Overheat. Coolant past the line: a takeover you cannot miss, until it cools or you snooze it.
  • Charging. Volts falling (alternator) or rising (regulator), with the battery chemistry's own limits.
  • Check engine. The code, what it means in plain words, and whether to stop soon or drive gently to a shop.
  • Oil pressure. The one alert that says stop the engine now.
  • Fridge and freezer. Warm, too cold, or thawing.
  • Storm. Pressure falling fast over three hours.
  • Towing. Transmission fluid climbing toward its ceiling on a loaded grade.
