THE KNOB

One knob does everything on the real clock. On this page the screen itself is the knob.

Turn to change pages

Scroll over the screen, or drag around the dial under it. Each click of the wheel is one notch of the real knob, through the real encoder code.

Click to open

Tap the screen. On a gauge page a click opens the big gauge; on the clock it steps through the corner widgets.

Hold for the menu

Keep your finger down. After the firmware's own long-press time the page's menu comes up: set the time, pick a face, choose widgets.

Weather from pressure

The clock keeps three days of barometer and warns of a storm. The demo drive carries real readings, so the trend is real.

Solar and the house battery

Watts in, amps, charge state. On the truck this comes over Bluetooth from the solar controller.

Health over weeks

Crank volts, charging, fuel trim, coolant and mileage, each a small graph of recent drives.

Trips and fuel

Two trip meters, the tank, and a fill-up prompt that learns where you buy fuel.

INJECT A FAULT

These are the firmware's own demo events. Each one bends a real reading, so the real alert fires on the clock. One at a time; each eases back by itself after about a minute.

TIP THE TRUCK

The clock has a tilt sensor for camp. Grab this truck and the TILT page follows, through the firmware's own filter.

Drag up and down for pitch, side to side for roll.

The needles settle over a second or two, the way they do in the truck. The camp view tells you which wheel to lift.
