  • Bluetooth OBD2 dongle included. Plug it in once and leave it.

FULL ENGINE DIAGNOSTICS

Full diagnostics on every drive. Shows active and pending codes as they appear, and saves freeze-frame data for every one.

  • Codes are explained in plain language, with a starting point for troubleshooting and a note on how serious they are.
  • The moment it happened, saved. Freeze frame on every code.
  • Live engine gauges. Transmission temp comes from the ECU on 1999 and newer automatics, or from an optional sender on older trucks.
  • Oil or fuel pressure, live and logged. Optional sender.
  • Smog check and code clearing.

Live engine data and trouble codes require the Bluetooth OBD2 dongle and a 1996 or newer truck.

ALERTS

Watches the whole time and takes over the screen for serious issues like high coolant or transmission temperature.

  • Over-voltage. Regulator failure alert.
  • Not charging. Alternator failure.
  • Serious engine codes. Critical codes, full screen.
  • Low oil pressure. With the optional sender: a stop-engine alert.
  • Notices gradual problems that would otherwise only show up after something fails.
  • Fridge and freezer. Custom temperature alerts.
  • Towing. Alerts for trans and coolant.

INTEGRATED GPS

Built-in GPS. Sets the clock and time zone automatically. No phone or subscription needed.

  • GPS ground speed. Accurate on any truck.
  • Compares GPS distance against the engine data so its mileage stays accurate.
  • Altitude, climb and grade. Barometer plus GPS, with total climb and descent.
  • Auto dimming. With the headlights, or at local sunset and sunrise.
  • Fuel stop detection. Prompts a fill log after you fill up.
  • GPS-based border crossing alerts.
  • Places and home. Saved places and trip summaries.

EVERY SECOND, LOGGED

Logs more than 70 readings every second of every drive, plus every alert. It's all on the SD card in the dash.

  • Every start, every code, every tank. Pre-start voltage and crank dip. Every code with freeze frame. Real gallons against real miles.
  • Wakes up on a schedule while the truck is parked to keep logging trends. Draws very little power and shuts off if the battery gets low.
  • Room for decades. 8 GB holds about 20 years at 1,500 miles a month. Takes up to a 32 GB card.
  • Spreadsheet-ready logs. Graph anything against anything.
  • Your drives on a map. Export any drive as KML or GPX for Google Earth or any map app.
  • Privacy first. One switch turns off location logging.

PURPOSE-BUILT HARDWARE

Custom board designed for these trucks. Parts chosen to handle dash heat and vibration.

  • Two processors so the screen stays responsive while it's logging everything.
  • A bright, sharp screen. Readable in sun, dim at night.
  • Built-in barometer. Weather trends, storm warnings, GPS-checked altitude.
  • Precision voltmeter. Resting, charging and cranking voltage, accurately measured.
  • Integrated gyro and accelerometer. Precise camp leveling and accurate off-road tilt.
  • Protected. Fused, surge-protected, reverse-polarity safe.
  • Designed and tested in Washington.

EXPANDABLE

Use it as-is or add probes and senders later. You decide how much you want on it.

  • Add sensors over time. Up to four probes, trans temp and oil or fuel pressure. Each appears as a gauge and in the logs once connected. See the senders it supports today.
  • Add solar when you're ready. Pair and go.
  • Easy Wi-Fi updates from your phone. New features added frequently.
  • Room to expand. Expansion port allows for future add-ons.

WHAT IT WON'T DO

  • Read-only. It never changes the truck's programming. You can clear codes, but only when you confirm it.
  • No cloud, no account, no subscription. Everything stays on the SD card in the dash.
  • No location logging unless you want it. One switch.
  • No live engine data before 1996. Engine data from OBD2 requires a dongle.
  • Won't drain your battery. Under 1 mA parked between wakes, with a low-battery cutoff built in. The numbers.
  • Not a mechanic. A head start.

WHY I BUILT THIS

I'm Derek. I've daily driven my 1997 4Runner SR5 since 2003: 410,000 miles, all of the lower 48 states, eight full laps around the country. I know these trucks inside and out.

It started with black ice I didn't see coming and a rooftop tent I could never get level. A lot of what's out there is a universal gauge, put in a new case and wedged in where the clock was. My one rule was factory fit: no changes to the dash, the factory mounting points and plug, and a screen that fills the factory bezel. Nothing like that existed, so I built it.

OVERCLOCK is me and my son, in small batches out of our garage in Vancouver, Washington. We hand-solder the connectors, print the cases, and assemble, test and pack every unit. It's open to tinkering too: a rescue mode and a library for the board let you run your own firmware.
