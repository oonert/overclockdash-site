Questions

The ones we get asked most. Something else? hello@overclockdash.com

Which trucks does it fit?

Third-generation 4Runners, first-generation Tacomas and T100s. It replaces the factory dash clock and plugs into its connector. In the works: the 1996 and 1997 Land Cruiser and Lexus LX 450 with the full feature set, and the 1st and 2nd gen 4Runner with GPS, weather, tilt, temperatures and logging but no engine pages. Want one of those? Say so on the interest form and we'll tell you when it's ready.

How hard is it to install?

Pop out the factory clock, plug OVERCLOCK into the same connector, and click it into the same opening. Then plug the Bluetooth adapter into the diagnostic port under the dash. No cutting or splicing.

Can I put the factory clock back?

Yes. Nothing is modified, so the original clock plugs right back in.

Does it work on trucks older than 1996?

Yes, with GPS, weather, tilt, temperatures, logging and everything else. Engine codes and live engine data need a 1996 or newer truck with the diagnostic port. Some 1995s have it too, so check under your dash.

Do I need the Bluetooth adapter?

Only for engine codes and engine data. One that we know works comes in the box. If you would rather use your own, nearly any Bluetooth Low Energy adapter will work. Older Bluetooth 3.0 or "classic" adapters will not.

Is it like a ScanGauge?

A ScanGauge plugs into the diagnostic port and shows live engine data on its own screen. OVERCLOCK sits in the clock spot, reads the same engine data over the adapter, and adds GPS, temperature probes, tilt, a flight-recorder log and alerts that take over the screen when something matters.

Why a knob and not a touchscreen?

Have you tried a touchscreen the size of a dash clock while driving? We did. You end up staring at a two-inch screen, hunting for a button, with your arm stretched across the dash. So we didn't build one. OVERCLOCK uses one knob, mounted wherever your hand already rests: turn to move between pages, click to open, hold to go back. Your eyes stay on the road and your hand stays put. It also gives the screen back to the information instead of to buttons, so every page can be bigger and clearer than a touchscreen could ever let it be. The knob comes with a flush mount, a stick-on housing and a five-foot sheathed cable that routes behind the dash, so it can go in the console, on the dash, by the shifter, or wherever suits your truck.

Can it change my engine settings or tune my truck?

No. It only reads. The only thing it ever changes is clearing engine codes, and only when you hold the knob to confirm.

Is there a subscription or an account?

No. No subscription, no account, no cloud. Everything stays in your truck.

Do I need my phone?

No. Everything works from the knob. Your phone is optional: turn on Wi-Fi and the dash opens a page on it for live data, logs, settings and updates. Nothing to download from an app store.

Are temperature probes included?

Two come in the box. The clock takes up to four, plus a transmission temperature sender and an oil or fuel pressure sender. More probes are available. See the senders it supports.

Can I read transmission temperature?

Yes. 1999 and newer automatics report it to the clock directly. Older trucks and manuals can add a supported sender. A manual doesn't need one; transmission temperature is mostly an automatic-transmission concern.

Does it work with my solar setup?

It works with Victron SmartSolar charge controllers and SmartShunt battery monitors today, plus many lithium batteries with Bluetooth built in. More brands are coming.

Will it drain my battery?

Driving, it draws about a tenth of an amp, about one dashboard bulb. Parked, it switches itself off and wakes for a 19-second check-in once an hour, which adds about 2 to 5 percent to what the truck already draws sitting still. If the battery runs low, it stops waking. The full numbers are on the power page.

What happens when the truck is off?

It saves your trip and shuts down about 18 seconds after the key. Then it wakes on a schedule you pick to check the probes, the weather and your solar, logs one line, and goes back off. So you can see how cold the cab got, or how warm the fridge got, overnight.

What happens if I disconnect the battery?

Your settings and logs are kept. The time resets, and GPS sets it again the next time you drive with a view of the sky.

Is the screen readable in daylight?

Yes. It's a bright, high-contrast screen that dims on its own at sunset or with your headlights.

How do I get updates?

Send the update file from your phone over Wi-Fi, or copy it to the memory card. The clock checks the file before installing.

What memory card should I use?

A name-brand microSD card from a trusted seller. Off-brand cards can fail in this kind of device.

Where does my data go?

Onto the memory card in the dash, and nowhere else. Our privacy page has the details.

Is it waterproof?

No. It lives in the dash like the clock it replaces.

What's the warranty and return policy?

Every unit comes with a one-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Dead on arrival? We replace it and cover the shipping. A defect in the first year is repaired or replaced. Damage from installation, water, or modifications isn't covered. Returns are accepted within 30 days in original condition. Full terms on the terms page.

Yes. It contains radio modules that carry their own FCC ID. See Regulatory Information.
