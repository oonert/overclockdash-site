Using the product

OVERCLOCK reads information from your truck and shows it to you. It does not tune, reprogram or control the truck. What it shows is a reading, not a diagnosis: a good mechanic still makes the final call, and a warning on the screen is a reason to check, not a guarantee that something is or is not wrong. Keep your eyes on the road; set it up while parked.
