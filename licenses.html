Fonts

OVERCLOCK's screens are set in TeX Gyre Heros, a typeface by B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki for the GUST e-foundry, based on URW Nimbus Sans. Used under the GUST Font License (GFL), a free license that permits commercial use and embedding. Copyright 2007 to 2009 by B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups).

The OVERCLOCK wordmark on the startup screen is set in Archivo Black by Omnibus-Type, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright 2012 The Archivo Black Project Authors.

Guide signs on the clock are set in Roboto Condensed by Christian Robertson for Google, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The digital numerals are drawn by OVERCLOCK's own segment renderer and are not a licensed typeface.
