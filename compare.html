OVERCLOCK vs ScanGauge and UltraGauge

A gauge shows you a number. OVERCLOCK is a field computer. It shows the numbers too, then it keeps working: it logs every drive, every second, watches for readings that are slowly getting worse, and lets you go back over any drive on your phone after the fact. ScanGauge and UltraGauge make good gauges. They're very good choices if you drive a Hyundai Sonata or a Jeep Wrangler. Just showing the number isn't enough.

Jump to: ScanGauge · UltraGauge

Thinking about a ScanGauge?

ScanGauge makes a capable engine monitor that fits almost any car built since 1996. OVERCLOCK is built for 3rd gen 4Runners, 1st gen Tacomas and T100s. It replaces the factory clock and watches the whole truck, not just the engine: GPS, temperature probes, battery and solar monitoring, tilt, weather alerts and a log of every drive, with no subscription. If you drive one of these trucks, we think OVERCLOCK is the better fit. Want one?

Thinking about an UltraGauge?

UltraGauge is a capable OBD2 gauge, popular with overlanders. It's a separate display on your dash or windshield with a cable to the diagnostic port. OVERCLOCK replaces your factory clock instead, and adds what a plug-in gauge can't.
