Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026.

What this site collects

Almost nothing. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies on overclockdash.com. The pages, fonts, pictures, 3D models and the browser demo are all served from this site itself, so nothing you do here is reported to anyone else.

The "Want one?" form

If you fill in the form (or write to us), we receive your email address, what you drive, and anything you add in the notes. We use it for one thing: to reply to you about OVERCLOCK and to keep our place-in-line list for the first batch. It is stored in a private spreadsheet in our Google Workspace account and in our mailbox. It is never sold, shared, or used for a mailing list. Email hello@overclockdash.com any time and we will delete your entry.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers see the ordinary technical details of each request (your IP address, browser type, the page requested) in their server logs. Their handling of that is described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or look at those logs.

The browser demo

The demo runs the real OVERCLOCK firmware inside your browser. It does not send anything anywhere; it does not even remember your settings between visits.

Children

This site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
