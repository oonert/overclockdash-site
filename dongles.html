Which OBD dongle?

We recommend the upgraded dongle. It shuts itself off when the truck is parked, so you can leave it plugged in. If you'd rather buy your own, here is how to pick one.

How to pick one

Most Bluetooth Low Energy OBD2 dongles work with OVERCLOCK. Every one we've tried has. Look for these on the box or the listing:

  • It says "Bluetooth Low Energy", "BLE" or "Bluetooth 4.0".
  • It works with both iPhone and Android.
  • It works with common apps like Torque, OBD Fusion or Car Scanner.

Skip dongles that only work with their maker's own app. They are locked to that app and won't talk to OVERCLOCK.

As a bonus, pick one that turns itself off when the truck is parked. The table shows which ones do.

Our pick

We recommend the Vgate iCar Pro BLE 4.0, or the iCar Pro 2S, which performed the same. Parked, they draw about a tenth of what the others do, so you can leave them plugged in. The upgraded dongle we offer is a Vgate iCar Pro.

If you buy an iCar Pro yourself, choose the "BLE 4.0" version. The same listing also offers BT3.0 and WiFi versions, and those are different dongles.

If yours doesn't turn itself off

Unplug the dongle if the truck will sit for more than about a week.

These are only the dongles we've tested so far. Others that match the list above should work too, and we'll add more to the table as we test them.
