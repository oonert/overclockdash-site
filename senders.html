Sender support

Add the gauges you want. These are the senders OVERCLOCK supports today. Pick yours in Settings and the gauge, alerts and logging set themselves up.

Temperature probes (two included, up to four)

  • Standard waterproof DS18B20-style digital temperature probes.
  • All probes share one screw terminal and are told apart automatically. Name each one: Inside, Outside, Fridge, Freezer, or your own.

Transmission temperature (one sender)

  • Auto Meter 2252
  • Auto Meter 2258
  • VDO 120 °C (250 °F) temperature senders
  • GM-style 2-wire temperature senders

One-wire and two-wire senders both work. One-wire senders need a short ground wire from the transmission case to the clock for an accurate reading.

On 1999 and newer automatic trucks, transmission temperature can also come straight from the engine computer with no sender at all.

Oil or fuel pressure (one sender)

  • Tested: AEM 30-2131-100 (100 psi, oil and fuel rated)
  • Works with standard 3-wire, 5-volt, 0.5 to 4.5 volt pressure senders in these ranges:
    Oil: 100, 150 or 200 psi, or 10 bar
    Fuel: 100 or 150 psi

Oil pressure zeroes itself every time you turn the key with the engine off.

More senders will be added over time. Don't see yours? Contact us.
